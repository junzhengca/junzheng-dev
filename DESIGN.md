# Jun Zheng personal site direction

## Identity

A personal homepage for a software engineer, for readers who want a quick sense of Jun's work and interests. Make it feel like a small, well-kept corner of the web: open, practical, and a little playful. Keep the voice plain and personal.

## Reference translation

The user pointed to Stoatify and Cap, then asked for stock-photo backgrounds and other AI startup references. Borrow the warmth of a product page and Cap's open sky. Granola's stacked note imagery and Runway's cinematic hero are useful examples of how other AI companies use layered visuals. Do not copy any site's layout, art, logo, or screenshots.

## Visual language

- Use a light, cool surface with soft blue and lilac panels, dark ink, and one steady blue accent. This keeps the page open and lets the real badge art add color.
- Use a readable system sans for body text and a familiar serif for headings. The serif gives the personal voice more warmth than the current oversized geometric display type.
- Keep the layout compact and easy to scan. A split introduction leads into interests and work history; the page should feel like a personal homepage rather than a studio portfolio.
- Use one local Unsplash cloud photo as the intro background. A cool, opaque wash keeps text readable in both themes. The photo sets the mood; it does not represent Jun. Credit the photographer in the footer.
- Use rounded panels for real groups of content: the intro, profile badges, and the software list. Give only the profile panels a light, tinted lift. Keep text lists on the page surface.
- Show the real TryHackMe and Hack The Box badges, the tools Jun recommends, and facts from the profile README. Do not add fake product screenshots, scores, testimonials, or other invented claims.
- Add the original pixel-art chibi male mascot requested by Jun as a small site companion. He holds a steaming Starbucks hot coffee. He idles with a gentle float and plays his walk cycle only while Jun drags him. He stays where Jun puts him and moves only when dragged or moved with arrow keys. Turn looping motion off when reduced motion is requested. Do not copy a named game character or use that game's franchise marks.
- Use a consistent set of small line icons to give the real links, interests, sections, and timeline more visual shape. Each icon should clarify the item beside it; do not use icons as random filler or add a wall of icon-only links.

## Motion

Dial: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Remove scroll choreography and large 3D rotations. Use short hover, focus, and pressed states to make controls feel responsive. The mascot floats while idle and walks only during a drag; pointer movement alone never moves it. Keep drag and keyboard movement available in every motion setting.

## Type and copy

Use simple sentences and keep the existing, accurate descriptions. Section labels should read like ordinary navigation, not a technical index. Avoid decorative labels that repeat the heading.

## Resilience

Start in light mode and keep the dark-mode control available. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, 200% text zoom, narrow screens, and reduced motion. Preserve image dimensions and descriptive alt text on the external badges. Navigation must point to real sections, with no horizontal overflow.

## Decision notes

- Color: cool white, blue, and lilac echo the references while keeping Jun's real badge colors distinct.
- Layout: a shorter split hero gives the person and current work equal footing without an oversized manifesto headline.
- Typography: a familiar serif headline softens the geometric, high-tech feel; a system sans keeps details readable.
- Spacing: tighter section spacing makes the site feel like a usable homepage rather than a long campaign page.
- Panels: rounded surfaces group profile details and badge art as real collections; the rest of the content stays flat.
- Photo: a real cloud image gives the hero the open, airy backdrop Jun asked for; the cool wash holds contrast without hiding the clouds.
- Depth: one restrained shadow separates the badge collection from the page and preserves the layered feel without another tilt effect.
